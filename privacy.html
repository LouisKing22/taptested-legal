Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

  1. Data from TikTok. The TikTok login grants basic profile access (user.info.basic). TapTested Engine does not read or store this profile data.
  2. Videos. Videos are created on your own computer. A video is uploaded to your TikTok inbox only when you start that step.
  3. Keys and tokens. Access keys and tokens are stored in a local file on your computer.
  4. Third parties. Pixabay receives your search terms, the clip IDs we request, our API key, and your computer's network address, as with any web request. TikTok receives the videos you upload.
  5. Sharing. We do not sell personal data.
  6. Retention and deletion. Data on your computer stays there until you delete it. Videos you upload to TikTok stay on TikTok until you delete them there. To disconnect, revoke access in your TikTok settings, then delete the TIKTOK_ACCESS_TOKEN and TIKTOK_REFRESH_TOKEN lines from .env.
  7. Your rights. You can ask what data we hold about you and ask for it to be deleted. Write to info@germanglobal.net.
  8. Contact. info@germanglobal.net
