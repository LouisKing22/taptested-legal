Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

  1. Data from TikTok. The TikTok login requests basic profile information (user ID, display name and avatar) under the user.info.basic permission. TapTested Engine uses it only to identify the connected account and does not store it on any server.
  2. Videos. Videos are created on your own computer. A video is uploaded to your TikTok inbox only when you start that step.
  3. Keys and tokens. Access keys and tokens are stored in a local file on your computer.
  4. Third parties. Stock media providers, such as Pixabay, receive only the search terms needed to find clips. TikTok receives the videos you upload.
  5. Sharing. We do not sell personal data.
  6. Retention and deletion. Data stays on your computer until you delete it. To disconnect, revoke access in your TikTok settings and delete the local token file.
  7. Your rights. You can ask what data we hold about you and ask for it to be deleted. Write to info@germanglobal.net.
  8. Contact. info@germanglobal.net
